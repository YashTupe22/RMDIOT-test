The Roadmap

Admission Journey

1

Eligibility Check

Verify minimum 35% aggregate in 10th Standard (SSC) with Science and Mathematics. Document verification starts at local facilitation centers.

2

DTE Registration

Register on the DTE Maharashtra portal and obtain your Application ID. Upload necessary documents for e-Scrutiny or Physical Scrutiny.

3

Option Entry

Fill out your preference of courses and colleges in the CAP rounds. Ensure RMDIT (Code: 6415) is your first choice for priority allocation.

4

Final Admission

Reporting to the institute after seat allotment. Fee payment and original document submission to finalize your enrollment.
